# Cover letter (arXiv)

Dear arXiv moderators,

Please consider the submission **"Verifiable Incentives: Identity-Bound Contracts and Collusion-Proof Delegation for Agent Commerce"** for cs.AI (cross-list cs.MA, cs.GT).

The paper studies incentive design for long-lived personal agents that act in commerce. It makes four contributions: (i) a formal model of verifiable delegation in which identity, authority, budget, and payment are contractible; (ii) three results on verification, collusion, and staking; (iii) an architecture (PAIR-Commerce) and a 60-task benchmark (AgentCommerce-Bench); and (iv) a chain of real-model experiments, including a null result for instruction-following agents, a positive result for economically motivated agents, a repeated economic sandbox, and a semi-field study on real merchant catalogs. It also releases a real-money sandbox implementation with a Stripe test-mode validation and a safety envelope; no live payment is claimed.

The manuscript is 24 pages with 8 tables and 6 figures. Code, prompts, raw results, and the benchmark are available at https://github.com/88z44v9mkf-bot/verifiable-incentives.

The work is original, is not under review at a journal, and has not been published elsewhere. The author is Jipu Su (Geely Automobile Group; City University of Hong Kong).
